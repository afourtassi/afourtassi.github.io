I am currently Maître de Conférences, HDR (equivalent to Associate Professor) in computer science at Aix-Marseille University, within the Natural Language Processing (NLP) group (TALEP).

I am also Deputy Director of Research at the Institute of Language, Communication and the Brain (ILCB).

I am the founder of the Computational Communicative Development team (CoCoDev), where we study how multimodal interaction shapes language learning in humans and machines.

I have a background in Mathematics and Physics (École Polytechnique), Computational Cognitive Science (École Normale Supérieure), and Experimental Psychology (Stanford University).
